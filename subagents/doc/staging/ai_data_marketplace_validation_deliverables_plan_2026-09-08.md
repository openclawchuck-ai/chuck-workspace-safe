# 90-Day Validation Deliverables — Build Plan

## Objective
Create an execution-ready concierge validation playbook and KPI workbook for a licensed AI-data marketplace focused on industrial maintenance and inspection data.

## Audience
Founder/operator, commercial lead, diligence lead, and external legal counsel.

## Playbook structure
1. Executive operating brief and clearly labeled assumptions
2. 13-week validation cadence, targets, roles, and decision gates
3. Buyer interview script
4. Seller interview script
5. Buyer brief template
6. Seller data-inventory questionnaire
7. Rights/provenance checklist with legal-review gates
8. Dataset passport template
9. Opportunity scoring rubric
10. Buyer and seller outreach copy
11. Diligence workflow, pilot structure, and source register

## KPI workbook structure
- README & assumptions
- Weekly KPI dashboard (13 weeks, targets, actuals, gap, status)
- Buyer pipeline and interview log
- Seller pipeline and interview log
- Dataset inventory and rights gates
- Match/diligence opportunities
- Pilot / transaction tracker
- Lists & definitions for consistent data entry

## Quality controls
- Restrained navy/teal business styling
- Explicit distinction between assumptions, evidence, and legal gates
- Source provenance retained in both artifacts
- DOCX ZIP/package validation and text-content checks
- XLSX load/recalculation/formula and sheet-structure validation
- PDF render and page-image inspection where local tools allow
