# AI Data Marketplace Launch Package — Render Plan

## Objective and audience
- Audience: Tony, founding team, and retained U.S. legal/tax advisers.
- Objective: convert the business-plan thesis into a decision-ready formation and launch operating package.
- Context: industrial maintenance and inspection data marketplace; concierge validation precedes scaled platform build.

## First artifacts
1. Formation action plan (DOCX): action queue, state decision gate, filing sequence, ownership/compliance gates, and 12-month calendar.
2. One-page brand option board (first slide of PPTX): three differentiated directions with names, positioning, palette, and risks.

## Parallel package
- AI-native operating system (DOCX): functions, agents, human gates, systems of record, workflows, controls, exceptions, logs, KPIs, 30-day cadence.
- Business administration framework (XLSX): chart of accounts, close checklist, registers, records, retention, naming/versioning, access matrix.
- Brand options (PPTX): one-page comparison plus detailed direction slides; names explicitly not legally cleared.
- Marketing plan (DOCX): 30-day activation and 12-month plan with funnel, campaigns, scenarios, CAC assumptions, KPIs, approval gates.
- Validation package: preserve and verify prior outputs; complete only if missing.

## Assumptions and decision gates
- U.S. domestic LLC; founder state and operating footprint are not yet confirmed.
- State selection is a documented decision gate, not an inferred choice.
- No legal, tax, trademark, privacy, insurance, or securities conclusion is final without qualified review.
- No filing, purchase, registration, application, or outreach is authorized by these documents.
- Dates and numerical budgets are planning assumptions and must be approved before commitment.

## Style and validation
- Restrained navy/teal/slate palette; strong hierarchy; concise action tables.
- Government/legal facts cited to primary sources where available; source notes preserved.
- Validate ZIP/package integrity and expected internal parts; open/render visually where existing tools support it.
