# Validation Package — Render Plan

## Objective
Run a 90-day concierge validation of a permissioned AI-data marketplace focused on industrial maintenance and inspection records.

## Playbook structure
1. Success criteria, assumptions, and human approval gates
2. Weekly 90-day cadence
3. Buyer interview script and credible-brief qualification
4. Seller interview script and data-inventory questionnaire
5. Buyer brief, rights/provenance checklist, and dataset-passport templates
6. Dataset/opportunity scoring rubric
7. Buyer and seller outreach copy
8. Decision rules, evidence standards, and sources

## Workbook structure
- Instructions
- KPI Dashboard
- Buyer Pipeline
- Provider Pipeline
- Dataset Register
- Match Diligence
- Experiment Log

## Metric definitions
- Buyer interview: completed discovery conversation with named organization/contact and dated notes.
- Credible buyer brief: documented use case, data requirements, permitted use, timeline, authority path, and budget signal confirmed by buyer.
- Seller interview: completed data-commercialization conversation with decision-maker or knowledgeable delegate.
- Rights-plausible dataset: preliminary evidence supports seller authority and no known fatal restriction; not legally cleared.
- Matched diligence opportunity: named buyer brief and dataset enter evidence-based diligence.
- Paid proof: executed terms plus collected or contractually committed consideration for a license or buyer-funded preparation pilot.

## Boundaries
- No raw dataset intake before privacy/security approval.
- No rights conclusion, external outreach, pricing commitment, contract, sample release, or payment without named human approval.
- Targets are validation goals, not forecasts.
