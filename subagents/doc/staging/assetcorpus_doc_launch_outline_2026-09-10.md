## Deliverable 1 — Rights Manifest + Dataset Passport v0.1

### A. Use instructions and control block

- One manifest/passport per candidate dataset or coherent collection.
- Version, dataset ID, prepared by, reviewer, review date, status, decision, and unresolved issues.
- Status vocabulary: Draft / Evidence pending / Under review / Conditionally acceptable / Accepted for approved discovery use / Rejected / Retired.
- Decision rule: unknown rights, authority, provenance, confidentiality, privacy, or custody facts remain explicit and block acceptance where material.

### B. Rights manifest

1. Dataset identity and parties
2. Ownership and claimed rights
3. Provider authority to grant or permit use
4. Provenance and acquisition history
5. Permitted uses and approved purpose
6. Restrictions, exclusions, geography, term, sublicensing, derivative works, model training, evaluation, publication, and redistribution
7. Privacy, confidentiality, personal information, sensitive operational information, export/security flags, and de-identification
8. Transformations already performed and transformations allowed
9. Retention, return, deletion, backup, and verification obligations
10. Chain of custody, storage locations, access events, transfers, integrity/hash records
11. Evidence register with reference IDs, document names, dates, custodians, locations, and verification notes
12. Reviewer checklist, exceptions, remediation, approval boundary, status, and sign-off

### C. Dataset passport

1. Dataset overview and intended discovery use
2. Modalities: images/video, UT A-scan/B-scan/C-scan, thickness grids, metadata, reports, annotations
3. Asset and inspection context: asset class, component, material, environment, corrosion mechanism, inspection method, equipment/probe, procedure, date, location granularity
4. Labeling: taxonomy, label source, annotator qualifications, ground truth, uncertainty, disagreements, missingness
5. Files/formats: inventory, naming, identifiers, encoding, compression, units, coordinate conventions, sidecars, checksums
6. Quality: completeness, readability, resolution/sampling, signal quality, duplicates, corruption, metadata coverage, leakage risk
7. Splits: unit of separation, train/validation/test allocation, asset/site/time/provider isolation, deduplication, holdout governance
8. Limitations and prohibited inferences
9. Acceptance criteria and pass/conditional/fail result
10. Change log and reviewer sign-off

### D. Acceptance gates

- Rights gate: ownership/authority and permitted use supported by evidence.
- Privacy/confidentiality gate: risks classified and controls documented.
- Provenance/custody gate: source and handling traceable.
- Technical gate: inventory opens, identifiers reconcile, quality thresholds recorded.
- Label gate: taxonomy and ground-truth basis documented.
- Split gate: leakage controls and split unit documented.
- Final gate: named reviewer records pass, conditional pass with remediation, or fail.

Only include decisions/actions that genuinely require Tony. Organize as NOW / NEXT / LATER. Every action includes:

- exact numbered steps
- estimated founder time
- prerequisite

### NEXT — decisions after internal evidence packets are ready

2. Review formation recommendation and separately approve or defer each external filing/purchase.
3. Review discovery target queues and approve named outreach batch, channel, script, and sender.

5. Review Gate 1 budget requests line by line.

### LATER — triggered only by evidence or milestone

2. Approve pilot terms, data-transfer method, and access controls.
